# Mailchimp Template - Image Specifications

## Required Image Dimensions

| Image Type | Width | Height | Notes |
|------------|-------|--------|-------|
| **Logo** | 150px | Auto | Fixed image, not editable. Maintains aspect ratio. |
| **Hero Image** | 600px | 300px | Top banner image. Editable in Mailchimp. |
| **Native Ad Image** | 560px | 315px | Blue background section. Editable in Mailchimp. |
| **Banner Ad** | 560px | 100px | Standard banner advertisement. Editable in Mailchimp. |
| **Editor Photo** | 130px | 130px | Square profile photo. Editable in Mailchimp. |
| **Article Content** | Variable | Variable | Articles use HTML editor - paste formatted content with inline images. |

## Template Variants

### Small Template
- 1 Hero Image (600x300)
- 1 Native Ad Image (560x315)
- 1 Banner Ad (560x100)
- 1 Editor Photo (130x130)

### Medium Template
- 1 Hero Image (600x300)
- 1 Native Ad Image (560x315)
- 3 Banner Ads (560x100 each)
- 1 Editor Photo (130x130)

### Big Template
- 1 Hero Image (600x300)
- 1 Native Ad Image (560x315)
- 4 Banner Ads (560x100 each)
- 1 Editor Photo (130x130)

### Extra Big Template
- 1 Hero Image (600x300)
- 2 Native Ad Images (560x315 each)
- 3 Banner Ads (560x100 each)
- 1 Editor Photo (130x130)

## Image Guidelines

1. **Format**: Use JPG or PNG format
2. **Optimization**: Compress images for email (keep file size under 200KB per image when possible)
3. **Aspect Ratio**: Maintain exact aspect ratios specified above
4. **Quality**: Use high-quality images that look sharp at specified dimensions
5. **Editor Photo**: Crop to exact 130x130px square before uploading for best results in all email clients

## Important Notes

- All images use a single URL (no conditional Outlook-specific URLs)
- Images are automatically responsive on mobile devices
- Native ad and banner images are 560px wide to align perfectly with text content (which has 20px padding on each side within the 600px container)
- The logo remains fixed and cannot be edited in Mailchimp campaigns
